Sub-Audible Speech Decoding & Linear Sequence Scaling

Deciphering sub-audible speech biosignals from surface electromyography (sEMG) represents an extreme signal-to-noise ratio challenge. Conventional Transformer architectures incur quadratic computational overhead on lengthy multi-channel biosignal recordings, making real-time inference untenable.

In collaboration with Amazon research scientists, we engineered and benchmarked modern State-Space Models (S5, Mamba) in PyTorch over multi-terabyte signal streams on high-performance computing clusters. By developing bespoke signal denoising and feature extraction pipelines, the model achieved a 15% absolute error rate reduction over standard acoustic baselines with strictly linear sequence recurrence complexity.
